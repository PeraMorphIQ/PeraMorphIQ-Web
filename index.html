Pioneering Neuromorphic Intelligence

PeraMorphIQ Neuromorphic Research Group stands at the forefront of brain-inspired computing innovation at the University of Peradeniya. We are dedicated to revolutionizing computational paradigms through comprehensive research spanning architecture design, algorithmic development, and system implementation.

Our interdisciplinary team conducts end-to-end development—from advanced ASIC implementations and sophisticated FPGA prototypes to cutting-edge simulation frameworks and groundbreaking algorithmic exploration. We specialize in developing configurable neuromorphic accelerators featuring on-chip learning capabilities, advanced memory optimization techniques, and real-time processing solutions.

By seamlessly bridging neuroscience, artificial intelligence, and embedded systems, we create intelligent computing platforms that embody the remarkable adaptability and energy efficiency of the human brain, paving the way for the next generation of cognitive computing systems.
