About the PeraMorphIQ Neuromorphic Research Group

PeraMorphIQ Neuromorphic Research Group at the University of Peradeniya advances the frontier of brain-inspired computation through comprehensive research in architecture, algorithms, and system design. The group conducts end-to-end development, from ASIC implementations and FPGA prototypes to simulation frameworks and algorithmic exploration. Our work focuses on configurable neuromorphic accelerators with on-chip learning, memory optimization, and real-time processing. By applying neuromorphic principles to solve computational challenges in a more energy-efficient manner, we bridge neuroscience, artificial intelligence, and embedded systems to create intelligent computing platforms that reflect the adaptability and efficiency of the human brain.
