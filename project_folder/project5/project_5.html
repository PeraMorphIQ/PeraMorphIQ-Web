On-Chip Learning for Spiking Neural Networks

Integrating learning capabilities into neuromorphic accelerators for adaptive hardware-based training

Technologies

Spiking Neural Networks
On-Chip Learning
Hardware Learning

The project focuses on integrating learning capabilities into neuromorphic accelerators to support both supervised and unsupervised training directly on hardware. By implementing on-chip and hardware-assisted learning mechanisms, this research aims to overcome the current limitations of neuromorphic systems that rely solely on offline training, bringing them closer to the adaptability of biological neural networks.

Advancing the frontiers of neural interface technology through innovative research and collaboration
