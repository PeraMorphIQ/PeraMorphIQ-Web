Configurable Neuromorphic Accelerator

Small-scale neuromorphic accelerator integrated within embedded SoC for real-time adaptable neural computation

Technologies

Neuromorphic Computing
Embedded Systems
SoC Integration
Accelerator Design

The project aims to design and integrate a small-scale neuromorphic accelerator within an embedded SoC, providing a bridge between software simulation and real hardware execution. The platform supports the development of end-to-end neuromorphic applications and serves as a foundation for future ASIC implementations that combine configurability, energy efficiency, and real-time adaptability.

Advancing the frontiers of neuromorphic computing through innovative research and collaboration
